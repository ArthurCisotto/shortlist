# Shortlist

Language for researching alternatives and reviewing their evidence.

## Language

**Candidate**:
A person, place, listing, or other alternative being considered for a search request.

**Must-have**:
A criterion whose confirmed failure excludes a candidate. Missing evidence leaves its status unknown.

**Preference**:
A criterion that improves a candidate's fit but whose failure does not exclude it.

**Criterion profile**:
The must-haves and preferences inferred from the user's request and confirmed by the user before research starts.

**Criterion status**:
Whether evidence supports that a candidate meets a criterion, fails it, leaves it unknown, or contains unresolved conflicting claims. Unknown and conflicting are distinct statuses.

**Card**:
A candidate's review view, showing its picture when available, criterion statuses, supporting sources, and relevant additional facts.

**Shortlist**:
The candidates the user marked Save.

**Refinement feedback**:
An explicit explanation of what the user wants more or less of in subsequent research. A Save/Pass decision alone does not supply that explanation.
