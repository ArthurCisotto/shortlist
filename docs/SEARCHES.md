# Searches Shortlist can help research

[← README](../README.md) · [Review and refinement examples](../EXAMPLES.md)

Shortlist can compare places, products, services, courses, and software. It is useful when the deciding details are scattered across listings, specifications, policies, schedules, and reviews.

These are hypothetical briefs you can adapt, not confirmed matches or recommendations. Some requirements may remain unknown after research. Use `$shortlist` in Codex or `/shortlist` in Claude Code.

## A rental for two couples and two dogs

```text
$shortlist Find holiday rentals for four adults and two dogs.
We need two enclosed bedrooms, each with a double bed, and two bathrooms
with separate showers. Both bedrooms must have air conditioning.
Each dog weighs 20 kg; verify the number and weight limits explicitly.
The yard must be private, fully enclosed, and not shared with other animals.
We also need two separate work surfaces for simultaneous laptop work.
Ask me for the destination, dates, and total budget including fees.
```

“Pet friendly” does not establish the permitted number or size of dogs. A listing advertising several units may show a bed inventory that applies to only one of them. Check the exact unit, detailed rules, and dated quote separately.

## One monitor for a laptop and desktop

```text
$shortlist Compare 27-inch 4K monitors sold in Brazil.
I need USB-C video with at least 65 W of laptop charging, a built-in KVM,
a separate USB upstream connection for the desktop, and at least two
USB-A downstream ports. Include a height-adjustable stand and a manufacturer
warranty valid in Brazil. Ask for my laptop model and delivered-price budget.
```

A USB-C port alone does not establish video input or charging power. Product-family descriptions can also mix specifications from different models. Look for the exact model's port table, KVM instructions, and regional warranty terms.

## Repairing an imported espresso machine

```text
$shortlist Find workshops that repair a US-bought Breville BES870XL, 120 V.
They must accept out-of-warranty imported equipment and handle both grinder
repair and pump replacement on this model. I need Saturday drop-off,
a written estimate before paid repairs, and a documented repair warranty.
Ask me for the city and maximum diagnostic fee.
```

Servicing a brand does not prove support for a particular model, voltage, or fault. Separate what the workshop advertises from what still needs a model-specific quote or confirmation.

## A pottery course with the whole cost included

```text
$shortlist Find a beginner pottery course with at least four in-person
sessions of two hours each, all on Saturdays. It must include pottery-wheel
instruction, with no more than eight students per class. The total must
include clay, glazing, and firing at least two pieces. Check whether one
missed class can be made up. Ask me for the city, start window, and budget.
```

A one-off wheel workshop is different from a multi-session course. Timetables, materials fees, firing allowances, and make-up policies may be published on separate pages.

## A notes app that works without a cloud account

```text
$shortlist Compare desktop notes apps for Linux. Creating and editing local
notes must work offline, with no cloud account required. The core app must
be free, and I need an officially documented Markdown export flow.
Optional paid synchronization is fine, but it must offer end-to-end
encryption. Show any setup conditions and unresolved export requirements.
```

Local storage does not by itself prove every operation works offline. Native Markdown files, backups, and an export command are different claims. Check detailed guides and alternative documentation routes when an initial help page fails.

## A museum visit with specific access requirements

```text
$shortlist Find London museums for an ordinary Saturday visit from 11 am
to 4 pm. Permanent-collection admission must be free, with a documented
step-free visitor entrance and accessible toilets. Walk-in entry without
a reservation must be explicitly allowed; capacity limits are acceptable.
Separate ordinary admission from paid exhibitions and special events.
```

A step-free route through the building is different from a usable entrance. Recommended booking is different from required booking. Ordinary hours do not settle holiday exceptions or access on a particular date.

## A coworking space for occasional video calls

```text
$shortlist Find coworking spaces offering single-day access without
membership. I need a phone booth that day-pass holders may actually use,
with reservations available and no extra booth charge for two hours.
The site must open by 8 am, have step-free access, and allow me to leave
and return the same day. Ask me for the city, date, and all-in budget.
```

A venue can advertise booths while restricting them to monthly members. Check the specific day-pass tier, booking limits, included hours, access route, and mandatory charges.

## A private dinner where the event terms matter

```text
$shortlist Find venues for a seated dinner for 18 people in a private room.
We need vegetarian mains, step-free access to both the room and a toilet,
and permission to bring our own cake. Compare the minimum spend, service
charge, room fee, deposit, and cancellation terms. Prefer a room without
music from another event. Ask me for the city, date, and total budget.
```

A restaurant's regular menu and opening hours may not describe its private-event package. Room capacity, cake fees, accessibility, and noise need evidence for the actual room and booking terms; quietness may remain uncertain.

## What to expect from a demanding brief

Shortlist keeps the criteria separate so a strong match on several details does not hide a decisive failure. It presents evidence and scope for each check, distinguishes missing information from inaccessible or ambiguous sources, and gives useful follow-up steps.

A search can produce partial matches or no eligible options. Candidates with confirmed must-have failures remain available in the collapsed **Excluded candidates** section, with their criteria and sources. You can review the evidence, save promising options, or refine the brief without turning an unknown into a confirmed match.
